Training provenance note

The EfficientSAM paper reports pretraining and fine-tuning involving
ImageNet-1K, SAM ViT-H features, and SA-1B. Those source datasets have terms
separate from the authors' Apache-2.0 publication of EfficientSAM. Custom Routes
does not distribute any training dataset.
