Custom Routes lets you create and share climbing routes from a gym photo.

Choose the holds in your route, assign start, finish, regular, and feet-only roles, then export a full-resolution JPEG with clear outlines. Projects remain editable so you can refine hold boundaries, change roles, undo edits, and return later.

You can mark holds manually or use optional on-device AI assistance. The AI model is downloaded only after you approve it and can be removed from the app's Privacy & Data settings.

Photos, projects, masks, preferences, and AI inference remain on your device. The app has no analytics, advertising, accounts, or backend service.
